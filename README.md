Rainy Season Diseases & Weather Impact Dashboard

Problem Statement

During the rainy season, many cities experience a surge in waterborne and vector-borne diseases such as dengue, malaria, and cholera. Weather conditions like temperature, humidity, and rainfall play a crucial role in the spread and survival rates of these diseases.
However, without proper monitoring and analysis, healthcare authorities often face challenges in **tracking disease cases, patient loads, and identifying risk zones**.

This project aims to:

* Analyze weather parameters (temperature, humidity, rainfall).
* Track city-wise disease cases and patient data.
* Compare survival vs diseased cases under different weather conditions.
* Provide interactive dashboards to support **data-driven healthcare decisions**.
